Privacy notice

SEASTRONG partner questionnaire. Version 1, October 2026.

This notice explains, in plain terms, what happens to the information you give in the SEASTRONG partner questionnaire.

Who is responsible

The SEASTRONG project, funded by the European Union’s Horizon Europe program under grant agreement No 101286483 and coordinated by the University of Bremen, Germany.

Contact for this questionnaire: Graham Patterson, SEASTRONG policy and communications, graham@reef-scuba.org.

What we collect

  • Your name, role and work email.
  • The work contact details of colleagues you list.
  • Your organization’s answers and comments.
  • Your organization’s social media accounts and approximate follower numbers.
  • The time you spent on the questionnaire and your time zone.

Why we collect it

To plan and build the SEASTRONG website and social media, and to coordinate with partner organizations.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. For colleagues you list, the legal basis is the project’s legitimate interest in coordinating its communications with partner organizations (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Who sees it

Individual responses are seen only by the SEASTRONG communications team and project coordination at the University of Bremen.

Everyone who completes the questionnaire sees vote totals, combined follower numbers by platform and the names of organizations that have responded. They never see names, contact details or comments.

Where it is stored

Responses are stored in a Supabase database hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

The questionnaire pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. in the United States, which processes visitors’ IP addresses to deliver the pages.

The questionnaire uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party fonts. Your progress is stored only in your own browser until you submit. After you submit, your browser keeps a copy of your own choices (not your contact details or comments) so the results can mark them. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

How long we keep it

Until it is no longer needed for the project’s communications, and no later than the end of the project on 28 February 2031. We delete contact details sooner if you ask us to.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of these, contact us at the address above.

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Bremen, Germany.

Funded by the European Union

SEASTRONG has received funding from the European Union’s Horizon Europe research and innovation programme under grant agreement No 101286483. Views and opinions expressed are however those of the author(s) only and do not necessarily reflect those of the European Union or the European Research Executive Agency (REA). Neither the European Union nor the granting authority can be held responsible for them.
